Our perspective

In a world built for AI, human communities matter more than ever.

The internet is racing toward AI-generated everything. We believe in a different future where people crave authentic conversation with real experts, real hobbyists and real people who share their obsessions. After all, imagine trying to complain to an AI about a bad call by the refs.

We believe forums are the last bastions of real authentic conversation on the internet and Forumly exists to make sure those communities don't just survive, they thrive.

Born from a real community's need.

Forumly didn't start in a boardroom; it started with a conversation. I met the owner of Reef2Reef through my previous company, and he asked me to build a dedicated mobile app for his community. In the process of building it, I realized two things:

1.

There are far more active, incredible forums on the internet than most people realize.

2.

Almost every forum running on XenForo shares the exact same core technical DNA and would benefit from a native mobile experience.

We're not building a one-size-fits-all aggregator, but a platform with hundreds of configurations so that each app is genuine to each community and suits their needs. Unlike Tapatalk or webview wrappers, every Forumly app is truly unique: their name, their icon, their App Store reviews, their relationship with their members. We just build it.
