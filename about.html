Our perspective

In a world built for AI, human communities matter more than ever.

The internet is racing toward AI-generated everything. We believe in a different future where people crave authentic conversation with real experts, real hobbyists and real people who share their obsessions.

We believe forums are the last bastions of real authentic conversation on the internet and Forumly exists to make sure those communities don't just survive, they thrive.

Imagine complaining to an AI about a bad call by the refs or bragging about the new mods you made to your car, it's just not the same.

Born from a real community's need.

The owner of Reef2Reef asked us to build a dedicated mobile app for his community. In the process of building it, we realized two things:

1.

There are far more active, incredible forums on the internet than most people realize.

2.

Almost every forum running on XenForo shares the exact same core technical DNA and would benefit from a native mobile experience.

We're not building a one-size-fits-all aggregator, but a platform with hundreds of configurations so that each app is genuine to each community and suits their needs. Unlike Tapatalk or webview wrappers, the apps we build for our Partner Forums are individually made. Your community and brand, we just build it.
