Getting Started

Before you begin

30 days from your first call to a live app in the App Store and Google Play. The timeline:   Week 1: Discovery call to understand your community and goals and go over our solution with you. Week 2: Technical and brand integration. You install our plugin, we input all of your app configurations and brand identity into the app. Week 3: Beta testing and fixes Week 4: App Store submission. Apple review typically takes 1–3 business days; Google Play is often faster.

Revenue & Pricing

The money questions

We measure your current mobile web ad revenue per user (your baseline ARPU). Once the app is live, any in-app ad revenue is split 50/50. Here's where we back it up, if the app doesn't perform above your baseline by 2x, we take nothing until it does (the 2× Guarantee). We only make money if we make you money. Revenue is reported monthly and your share is paid out directly.
For forums with 500+ DAUs on the standard plan, the $6,000 setup fee is typically recovered within 3–6 months. After that, the 50/50 split is pure additional revenue above and beyond what your mobile web Users are already generating since we take nothing if we don't double your ARPU.
Premium stays premium. Any member with an existing paid/ad-free subscription will never see ads in the Forumly app. Their experience is respected. Their tier carries over seamlessly via their User Groups.
Technical

Under the hood

We support the core XenForo feature set and many popular plugins. During the discovery call we'll go through your specific configuration and tell you exactly what's supported and what our roadmap looks like. Custom or niche add-ons may require additional scoping.
We integrate via the XenForo REST API plus a lightweight add-on installed on your forum. This handles real-time data sync, permissions, search, and notifications. All content lives on your servers, we are simply reading and writing through the API.
The app uses its own native UI/UX, it doesn't inherit your XenForo theme. So custom CSS or XenForo visual modifications don't carry over. Your branding (logo, colors, identity) is applied natively within the app. We parse all BBCode into native iOS and Android UI elements. For custom plugins, we assess on a case-by-case basis during the discovery call.
Yes. All data stays on your servers. We never store your forum's on Forumly infrastructure. Our add-on reads data through the XenForo API using your existing permission model. We follow industry standard security practices.

Other questions

If you already have a custom native app, we'd want to understand what you have and have an honest conversation about whether we can improve on it.
Night and day. Tapatalk is an aggregator: your community lives inside their app, under their brand, with their reviews and their relationship with your members. Forumly builds your app: your name in the App Store, your icon, your screenshots, your push notifications. All engagement accrues to your property. App Store & Compliance

Publishing & platform rules

We handle everything. Forumly manages the full submission process for both the Apple App Store and Google Play including all metadata, screenshots, descriptions with your input. The app is published under your name and brand, not ours. Your community gets the listing, we handle the logistics.
We stay on top of platform policy changes as part of our ongoing management. If Apple or Google update their guidelines in a way that affects your app, we handle the technical and submission response. That's a part of why operators partner with us rather than building in-house.
