New Feature

Live Mode

Some threads move fast with posts coming in a mile a minute. Game day threads, breaking news, live events. Live Mode is a feature we implemented to stream new posts into the thread as they're made, with no manual refreshing. Your Users stay in the conversation without losing their place or having to refresh.

Perfect for sports communities, event threads or any moment your community wants to experience together, in real time.
